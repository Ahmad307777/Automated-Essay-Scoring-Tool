Essay 1: Climate Change
STRONG
Climate change represents one of the most pressing challenges facing humanity today. Scientific evidence overwhelmingly demonstrates that human activities, particularly the burning of fossil fuels, are causing unprecedented global temperature rises. The consequences of inaction are dire, including rising sea levels, extreme weather events, and ecosystem collapse. Governments and corporations must take immediate, decisive action to address this crisis. For instance, transitioning to renewable energy sources like solar and wind power can dramatically reduce carbon emissions. Furthermore, reforestation programs and sustainable agricultural practices offer complementary solutions that can help absorb existing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. However, economic interests often hinder progress toward meaningful climate action. Therefore, international cooperation frameworks such as the Paris Agreement are essential to align national policies and ensure accountability. Developed nations must also provide financial and technological support to developing countries to facilitate their transition to clean energy. In conclusion, the scientific evidence unequivocally demands urgent, coordinated global action to avert catastrophic and irreversible climate consequences. The time for debate has passed; now is the time for action.

Essay 4: Importance of Reading
STRONG
Reading is fundamental to personal and intellectual development. Through books, individuals gain access to diverse perspectives, historical knowledge, and imaginative worlds that expand their understanding of humanity. Moreover, regular reading enhances vocabulary, critical thinking skills, and empathy. Research consistently demonstrates that students who read frequently perform better academically across all subjects. Reading comprehension skills are essential not only for literature classes but also for understanding scientific texts, mathematical word problems, and historical documents. Consequently, educators must prioritize reading instruction and encourage independent reading habits. Furthermore, reading serves as a powerful tool for social mobility and lifelong learning. In an increasingly complex world, the ability to analyze and synthesize written information is crucial for professional success. Libraries and digital platforms have made literature more accessible than ever before, democratizing knowledge and opportunity. In conclusion, cultivating a reading culture benefits individuals and society as a whole. Parents, teachers, and policymakers should work together to promote reading as an essential life skill and source of enrichment.

Essay 5: Social Media Effects
AVERAGE
Social media affects teenagers in both positive and negative ways. It helps them stay connected with friends and family. They can share photos and updates about their lives easily. However, spending too much time on social media can be harmful. Some studies show it can lead to anxiety and depression. Teenagers might compare themselves to others and feel bad about their own lives. Cyberbullying is another problem on social media. Some people say mean things online that they would never say in person. Parents should monitor their children's social media use and talk to them about online safety. Overall, social media is a tool that needs to be used responsibly. It has benefits but also risks that everyone should be aware of.
